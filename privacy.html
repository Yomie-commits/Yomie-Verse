Who I am

This website is operated by Naomi Mwangi, based in Nairobi, Kenya. You can contact me by email or on WhatsApp (+254 792 002 230).

What I collect

When you send an enquiry, I receive the details you enter: your name, business name, email address, phone number, the service you're interested in, your description of the challenge and your preferred contact method.

How I use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, arrange consultations or demos, and prepare proposals. I don't sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

How it is delivered and stored

Form submissions are delivered to my email inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). I keep enquiries only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records.

Analytics

If website analytics are switched on, I use aggregated statistics to understand which pages are useful.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time by contacting me.

Client automation projects

Automations I build for clients run in the client's own accounts. What data is processed, what access is granted and how long data is kept are agreed with each client before work begins.
