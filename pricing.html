How pricing works

Final pricing depends on workflow complexity, the number of integrations, expected usage and implementation requirements. You receive a written quote before any work starts.

Third-party tool costs (for example Make.com plans, which are charged by usage) are separate and paid directly by you. We help you choose the right plan; usage is never unlimited.

Pricing questions

Is the price a one-off fee?

Yes. Package prices cover design, setup, testing and handover. Monthly maintenance is optional and quoted separately.

What other costs should I expect?

Tools like Make.com charge based on how much the automation runs. For many small businesses this is a modest monthly amount; we estimate it with you during the assessment.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Many clients start with Starter and add drafting, tasks and calendar features later. Work already done carries over.

Do you accept international clients?

Yes. We can quote in KES or an equivalent amount in your currency.
