Pricing FAQ

Pricing questions

Is the price a one-off fee?

Yes. Package prices cover design, setup, testing and handover. Monthly maintenance is optional and quoted separately.

What other costs should I expect?

Tools such as Make.com charge based on how much the automation runs, and some AI features have usage costs. For many small businesses this is a modest monthly amount; we estimate it with you before building. Usage is never unlimited.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Many clients start with Starter and add drafting, tasks and calendar features later. Work already done carries over.

How are LeadFlow AI and custom projects priced?

They're quoted after a consultation, because the work depends on your lead sources, tools and volumes. You receive a written quote before anything starts.

Do you accept international clients?

Yes. We can quote in KES or an equivalent amount in your currency.
