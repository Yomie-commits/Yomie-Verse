What the system does

Eight capabilities working together to turn a busy inbox into a clear plan.

Monitors incoming emails

Checks your Gmail inbox on a schedule you choose, skipping spam, promotions and newsletters so only real mail is processed.

Summarises messages

Every email gets a short plain-English summary, so you can understand it without opening the full thread.

Detects priority

Each email is rated Critical, High, Normal or Low. Urgent items are labelled in Gmail and highlighted in your briefing.

Extracts actionable tasks

Required actions and stated deadlines become tasks with due dates in your task list.

Prepares email responses

When a reply is needed, a draft matching the tone of the original is saved inside the Gmail thread. Decisions only you can make are marked in [brackets].

Tracks follow-ups

Conversations that need checking back on get a follow-up date. Overdue items are flagged once, not repeated every day.

Supports calendar management

Meeting requests are detected, checked against your Google Calendar for conflicts and added only after you approve them.

Generates daily briefings

Each morning you receive a 7-section briefing: important emails, urgent matters, open tasks, deadlines, meetings, overdue follow-ups and recommended actions.

Gmail

Where your emails arrive and where reply drafts are saved, ready for you to edit and send.

Make.com

The automation engine. It runs on a schedule, connects your apps and moves information between them.

AI processing

Reads each email and returns structured information: summary, priority, actions, dates and a draft reply.

Google Sheets

Your dashboard: every email, task, follow-up and approval in one spreadsheet you own and can edit.

Google Calendar

Used to check your availability and, after approval, to add confirmed meetings.

Human approval

You decide. Drafts are only sent by you; events are only created when you mark them Approved.

Designed to be safe and dependable

  • No automatic sending. Replies stay as drafts until you send them.
  • No surprise bookings. Calendar events need your approval.
  • No duplicates. Each email is processed once, even if the system runs again.
  • No invented details. The AI is instructed to record only deadlines and commitments stated in the email.
  • Error logging. Problems are logged and retried, and you are alerted if something fails.
  • Your accounts, your data. Everything runs in your own Google and Make.com accounts.
