The problem

Enquiries slip through the cracks

Leads arrive by website form, email and WhatsApp. Without a system some are missed, replies are slow, and follow-ups depend on someone remembering.

Every missed or late reply is a potential client who may choose someone else. LeadFlow AI gives every enquiry a place, a priority and a next step.

What changes

  • Every enquiry is captured and logged automatically
  • You see which leads need attention first
  • Follow-ups happen on time, not when someone remembers
  • A clear weekly view of your pipeline
Capabilities

What LeadFlow AI does

Website enquiry capture

Enquiries from your website forms and inbox are collected automatically, in one place.

Organisation and categorisation

Each lead is labelled by service, source and urgency, so you can see what's coming in at a glance.

AI-assisted qualification

AI reads the enquiry and notes the need, budget signals and timeline to help you prioritise. You make the final call.

CRM and spreadsheet integration

Leads are logged to Google Sheets or a CRM you already use, with no copy-pasting.

Follow-up reminders

Every lead gets a follow-up date, and you're reminded before anyone goes cold.

Lead notifications

New and high-priority enquiries alert you by email straight away.

Pipeline reporting

A simple weekly view of new, contacted, qualified and won leads.

Questions
Where do the leads come from?

Usually your website forms and your business inbox. Other sources, such as Google Forms or other form tools, can often be added; we confirm this during the consultation.

Do I need a CRM?

No. Many clients start with a Google Sheets lead tracker, which is free and easy to use. If you already use a CRM, we check whether it can be connected.

Does the AI reply to leads by itself?

Not by default. LeadFlow AI can prepare a first-response draft, but you review and send it. Automatic replies can be considered for simple acknowledgements if you want them.

What does it cost?

LeadFlow AI is quoted after a consultation, because it depends on your lead sources and how many leads you receive. Tool usage costs, such as Make.com, are explained upfront.
