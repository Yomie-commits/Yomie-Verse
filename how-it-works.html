How it works

Five clear steps from first conversation to a working system

A transparent process that starts with your business, not the technology. You'll know what's being built, why, and what it will cost before any work begins.

Start with a consultation
1

Discover

Understand your business and challenges.

  • A conversation about how your business runs today
  • Where time is lost and what frustrates your team
  • Your goals, tools, budget and any constraints
2

Analyse

Identify repetitive tasks and automation opportunities.

  • Map the tasks that repeat every day or week
  • Estimate which automations will save the most time
  • Flag anything that should stay manual
3

Design

Plan a suitable workflow.

  • A written workflow plan you can understand
  • Clear scope, timeline and quote before work starts
  • Agreement on data, access and human approval points
4

Implement

Build, integrate and test the solution.

  • Built in your own accounts (e.g. Google Workspace, Make.com)
  • Tested with realistic examples before going live
  • Handover with simple documentation and training
5

Optimise

Monitor performance and improve the workflow.

  • Check results and fix anything that needs adjusting
  • Improve prompts, rules and reports as you learn
  • Add the next workflow when you're ready
What we need from you

Easy to get started

  • A 30–60 minute conversation about your work
  • Examples of the emails, enquiries or tasks you want automated
  • Access to the relevant accounts, granted securely when we build
  • Your feedback during testing
How we work

Our principles

  • Practical first: automate what genuinely saves time
  • Human in the loop: approvals wherever decisions matter
  • Transparent costs: including third-party tool costs
  • Your accounts, your data: systems run in tools you own
  • Documented: you understand what was built
