How it works

A clear, step-by-step process

From the first conversation to a tested, documented system, here is what working with YomieVerse looks like.

Discovery

We learn how your business runs today: the tools you use, where work comes in and where time is being lost.

Assessment

We identify the repetitive tasks best suited to automation and estimate the time saved and any running costs.

Design

We map a customised solution: what triggers it, what it does at each step and where you approve decisions.

Implementation

We connect the necessary applications (Gmail, Google Workspace, Make.com and others) in your own accounts.

Testing

We run the workflow with realistic test data and check every outcome before it goes live.

Handover

You receive training and clear documentation covering how to use, pause and adjust the system.

Ongoing support

Optional monitoring and improvements as your business and processes change.

What you can expect

Your tools, your data

Systems are built in accounts you own. You can see, change or switch them off at any time.

Tested before launch

Every workflow is tested with sample data, and results are shared with you before going live.

Plain-language documentation

A written guide explains daily use, troubleshooting and what each part of the system does.

A short conversation is enough to see where automation would make the biggest difference.
