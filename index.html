FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What exactly do you automate?

Repetitive admin and communication work: sorting and summarising email, turning requests into tasks, tracking follow-ups and deadlines, logging enquiries, checking calendars and preparing daily summaries. We start with the processes that cost you the most time.

Will the system send emails or book meetings on its own?

No. By design, replies are saved as drafts for you to review and send, and calendar events are only created after you approve them. You stay in control of everything that leaves your business.

Which tools do you work with?

Mainly Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Calendar and Make.com, with AI processing for summaries and drafting. Other tools can often be connected; we confirm this during the assessment.

Is my data safe?

The automation runs in your own Google and Make.com accounts, so your data stays in tools you control. We use only the access each workflow needs and you can revoke it at any time.

Are there running costs after setup?

Usually, yes. Tools such as Make.com charge by usage, so costs depend on how many emails or records you process. We estimate this with you before building so there are no surprises.

How long does a setup take?

A focused system such as email triage typically takes days rather than weeks. Larger, multi-step workflows take longer; you get a clear timeline after the assessment.

Do you work with businesses outside Kenya?

Yes. We work remotely with clients in Kenya and internationally, and can quote in KES or another currency.
