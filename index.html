FAQ

Questions businesses ask me

What do you do?

I help businesses reduce repetitive work by setting up practical AI automation: organising email and tasks, capturing and following up on leads, planning and approving social media content, and connecting the tools you already use.

Are your products apps I sign up for?

No. SocialFlow AI, ExecutiveFlow AI and LeadFlow AI are done-for-you systems that I design and set up for your business, mostly in tools you already own such as Google Workspace, together with Make.com and AI. There is nothing to install.

Will anything be sent or posted without my approval?

Not unless you want it to be. By default, email replies stay as drafts, social posts wait for approval and meetings are only added to your calendar once you confirm them.

Which tools do you work with?

Mainly Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Google Forms and Make.com, with AI for summaries, classification and drafting. Other tools can often be connected; I confirm this during the consultation.

How much does it cost?

Each product has its own pricing: ExecutiveFlow AI setups start at KES 15,000, SocialFlow AI lists its plans on its own site, and LeadFlow AI and custom projects are quoted after a consultation. You always get a written quote before work starts, and third-party tool costs such as Make.com are explained upfront. See pricing.

Do you work with businesses outside Kenya?

Yes. I work remotely with clients in Kenya and internationally, and can quote in KES or another currency.
