Services

Automation services for busy teams

Six ways we help businesses reduce repetitive work, respond faster and keep track of what matters, each built in tools you already use.

AI Executive Assistant Systems

Discuss this service

The business problem

Important emails get lost among routine messages. Deadlines hide in long threads, replies are delayed and nobody has a clear view of what is still open.

The solution

An AI assistant that monitors your inbox, summarises and prioritises each email, extracts tasks and deadlines, drafts replies and sends you a daily briefing.

How the automation works

Make.com checks Gmail on a schedule. AI reads each new email and returns structured information (priority, action, deadline, draft reply). Results are logged to Google Sheets, drafts are saved in Gmail and meeting requests are checked against Google Calendar for your approval.

Expected benefits

  • Hours saved each week on inbox triage
  • Urgent issues surfaced immediately
  • Consistent, professional replies prepared in advance
  • A single source of truth for open work

Email Management Automation

Discuss this service

The business problem

Shared and personal inboxes fill up with a mix of client mail, notifications and newsletters, making it slow to find what needs action.

The solution

Rules and AI working together to filter noise, label and summarise important messages and route them to the right place.

How the automation works

Incoming emails are filtered by category and sender. Relevant messages are summarised and labelled, and key details are logged to a sheet or forwarded to the right person or channel.

Expected benefits

  • A cleaner, prioritised inbox
  • Less time reading low-value email
  • Important messages never buried
  • Clear records of what came in and when

Client and Lead Management

Discuss this service

The business problem

Enquiries arrive by email, website forms and messages. Some get missed, follow-ups are forgotten and there is no record of where each lead stands.

The solution

An automated enquiry pipeline that captures every lead, records the details, prepares a first response and schedules follow-ups.

How the automation works

Form submissions and enquiry emails are captured automatically, details are extracted and added to a lead tracker, a reply draft is prepared and follow-up reminders are set based on the conversation.

Expected benefits

  • Every enquiry captured and tracked
  • Faster first responses
  • Follow-ups that happen on time
  • Visibility of your pipeline at a glance

Administrative Workflow Automation

Discuss this service

The business problem

Copying information between emails, forms and spreadsheets, preparing routine reports and chasing updates takes hours of manual effort.

The solution

Automated workflows that move data between your tools, keep records up to date and produce routine summaries.

How the automation works

Make.com connects your apps (Gmail, Google Sheets, Forms, Drive and others). Triggers such as a new email, form or row start a sequence of steps that formats, records and reports the information.

Expected benefits

  • Less manual data entry and fewer errors
  • Up-to-date records without chasing
  • Routine reports generated automatically
  • Staff time freed for higher-value work

Calendar and Task Automation

Discuss this service

The business problem

Meeting requests and tasks come in through email, but turning them into calendar events and to-dos is manual and easy to forget.

The solution

Automation that detects meeting requests and action items, checks your availability and prepares calendar events and tasks for your approval.

How the automation works

AI extracts proposed dates, times and actions from messages. The system checks Google Calendar for conflicts, lists the proposed event for approval and creates it only once you confirm. Tasks are logged with due dates and flagged as they approach.

Expected benefits

  • No double-bookings from email requests
  • Tasks captured with clear due dates
  • Reminders before deadlines, not after
  • Full control through simple approvals

Custom Business Automation

Discuss this service

The business problem

Every business has processes that do not fit an off-the-shelf tool, so they stay manual and slow.

The solution

A tailored automation designed around your process, built with Make.com integrations and AI where it adds value.

How the automation works

We map your current process, identify the steps that can be automated, then design, build and test a workflow connecting the tools you use, with error handling and logging.

Expected benefits

  • Automation that fits how you actually work
  • Connected tools instead of copy-paste
  • Reliable, documented workflows
  • A foundation you can extend later
