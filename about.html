About

Technology that makes work simpler

YomieVerse helps businesses use practical AI and automation to simplify operations and work more efficiently.

Hello, I'm Naomi Mwangi

I'm an AI Automation Specialist based in Nairobi, Kenya, and the founder of YomieVerse. My focus is helping businesses use technology to simplify operations, so that people spend less time on repetitive admin and more time on the work that grows the business.

My background brings together several areas that shape how I build automation:

  • Information and Communication Technology: Diploma in ICT
  • Web development: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap
  • Digital marketing and social media management
  • Virtual assistance and administrative support
  • AI data training and annotation
  • Business process automation with Make.com and Google Workspace

Having worked on the administrative side of businesses, I design systems around real day-to-day work, with people kept in control of the decisions that matter. I work remotely with clients in Kenya and internationally.
