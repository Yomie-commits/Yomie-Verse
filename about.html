About me

Making practical AI automation accessible to growing businesses

I believe AI shouldn't be reserved for large companies with big technology budgets. It should help every business work smarter, save time and grow.

My mission

Why I do this work

My mission is to make practical AI automation accessible to growing businesses, so their people spend less time on repetitive work and more time on growth.

Many entrepreneurs and small teams spend hours each week on work that follows the same pattern: answering similar emails, chasing enquiries, preparing posts and updating spreadsheets. AI and automation can now handle much of that, but the tools can feel complicated and out of reach.

I close that gap. I turn practical AI into simple, reliable systems built around how each business already works, so people spend less time on repetitive tasks and more time on the work that grows the business.

My vision

Where I'm going

A future where every growing business, whatever its size, can use AI as an everyday tool rather than an experiment.

  • Solutions that are useful from the first week
  • Technology explained in plain language
  • People in control of the decisions that matter
  • Systems clients own and understand
