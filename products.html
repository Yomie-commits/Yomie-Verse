Our products

Three AI products for the work that slows you down

SocialFlow AI, ExecutiveFlow AI and LeadFlow AI are independent products, each with its own website, features and offering. Choose one to start, and add the others as your needs grow.

Book a consultationRequest a demo

How our products are delivered. Each product has its own website where you can explore it in depth. Each product is a done-for-you system that we design and set up for your business, mostly inside tools you already own (such as Google Workspace), connected with Make.com and AI. There's no app to download, and you receive training and documentation at handover.

SocialFlow AI

Available now

Plan, create and approve social content in one flow.

An AI-powered social media workflow that turns your goals into a content calendar, drafts on-brand captions and hashtags, routes posts for approval and summarises how your content performs.

The problem it solves
Posting consistently takes hours of planning, writing and back-and-forth approvals, so content ends up last-minute or stops altogether.
Key capabilities
  • AI-assisted content generation
  • Social media content calendars
  • Caption and hashtag generation
  • Content strategy development
  • Content approval workflows
  • Publishing and scheduling integrations
  • Social media performance summaries
Ideal for
Businesses, social media managers, marketing agencies and entrepreneurs.

ExecutiveFlow AI

Available as a custom setup

Your inbox, tasks and meetings, organised every morning.

An AI-powered executive assistant that organises and summarises your email, turns requests into tasks, coordinates meetings, drafts replies for your approval and sends you a daily briefing.

The problem it solves
Important emails get buried, deadlines hide in long threads and follow-ups slip, while hours go into sorting the inbox instead of running the business.
Key capabilities
  • Email organisation and summarisation
  • Task extraction
  • Calendar and meeting coordination
  • Administrative workflow automation
  • Daily briefings
  • Follow-up reminders
  • AI-assisted email drafting with human approval
Ideal for
Entrepreneurs, executives, consultants and busy professionals.
Explore ExecutiveFlow AI Request a demo

LeadFlow AI

Available as a custom setup

Capture every enquiry and follow up on time.

An intelligent lead management system that captures enquiries from your website and inbox, organises and qualifies them, reminds you to follow up and shows where every lead stands.

The problem it solves
Enquiries arrive through forms, email and WhatsApp. Without a system some are missed, replies are slow and follow-ups depend on memory.
Key capabilities
  • Website enquiry capture
  • Lead organisation and categorisation
  • AI-assisted lead qualification
  • CRM and spreadsheet integration
  • Automated follow-up reminders
  • Lead notifications
  • Sales pipeline reporting
Ideal for
Service businesses, real estate companies, consultants, agencies and sales teams.
